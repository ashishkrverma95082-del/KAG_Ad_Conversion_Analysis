# Facebook Ads Performance Analysis

**PGDM Project | Business Analytics and Marketing**

## About the Project

I applied what I learned about campaign reporting during my Digital Marketing internship to analyze 1,143 Facebook ads across three campaigns. The goal was to compare ad spend, conversions, and cost per approved conversion.

## Key Findings

- Campaign 916 had the lowest cost per approved conversion (6.24).
- Campaign 1178 generated the most approved conversions (872), with a cost per approved conversion of 63.83.
- I would test a small budget increase for Campaign 916 to see if its cost efficiency holds at higher spend.

## Tools and Dataset

- **Tools** — Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Google Colab
- **Dataset** — [Sales Conversion Optimization on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/loveall/clicks-conversion-tracking)

## How to Run

Open the `.ipynb` notebook in Google Colab, select **Runtime → Run all**, and upload `KAG_conversion_data.csv` when prompted.
